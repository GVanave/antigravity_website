Your gateway to world-class public universities in Germany with zero tuition fees.

The Academic Pathway

Studying in Germany is globally recognized as a standard of excellence. Engineering, Management, and IT degrees from German public universities open doors to leading global companies.

What We Offer

  • University Shortlisting: We match your academic profile with the right public or private universities.
  • APS Certification & uni-assist: We handle the complex bureaucratic documentation required for Indian students.
  • Visa & Blocked Account Setup: Step-by-step guidance to secure your student visa and open a blocked account for living expenses.
  • SOP & LOR Writing: Professional guidance to make your application stand out.

Requirements

For a Bachelor's degree, students typically require 13 years of education (e.g., 12th standard + 1 year of university, or clearing JEE Advanced), or a foundation course (Studienkolleg). For Master's degrees, a recognized 3- or 4-year undergraduate degree is required along with English proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL) or basic German.
