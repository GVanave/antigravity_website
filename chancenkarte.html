Chancenkarte

The Opportunity Card is Germany's new points-based visa for skilled professionals to enter and search for work.

The Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)

Introduced recently to combat the skilled labor shortage, the Chancenkarte allows qualified non-EU professionals to enter Germany for up to one year to find employment. The biggest advantage? You do not need a pre-existing job offer or employment contract before you arrive.

Key Benefits

  • No Job Offer Needed: Search for jobs locally and attend in-person interviews.
  • Part-time Work Allowed: You are permitted to work up to 20 hours per week in any job to support yourself financially during your search.
  • Trial Work: You can engage in up to two weeks of trial employment per company.
  • Transition to EU Blue Card: Once you secure a qualified job, you can easily transition your visa status from within Germany.

Eligibility (The Points System)

You either qualify directly if your foreign degree is fully recognized in Germany, or you must score at least 6 points in the points-based system. Points are awarded for:

  • Language skills (German A1+ or English B2+).
  • Professional experience related to your qualification.
  • Age (preference for younger candidates).
  • Previous stays in Germany.

You must also prove you have sufficient financial resources to support yourself (approx. €1,091 per month).
