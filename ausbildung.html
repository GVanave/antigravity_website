Ausbildung

Germany's world-renowned dual vocational training system. Earn a salary while you learn a profession.

Earn While You Learn

The Ausbildung system is highly respected in Germany. It allows you to split your time between practical work at a company and theoretical study at a vocational school (Berufsschule). This means you earn a monthly stipend (approx. €850 to €1,200+) right from day one, with zero tuition fees.

Popular Industries

Germany faces a massive shortage of skilled workers in several sectors, making it highly advantageous for international candidates to apply in:

  • Healthcare & Nursing: A rapidly growing sector with high stipends and guaranteed job placement.
  • IT & Software Development: Learn on the job and get hired immediately upon graduation.
  • Mechatronics & Engineering: Hands-on training in Germany's legendary industrial sector.
  • Hospitality & Culinary Arts: Ideal for those wanting to manage world-class hotels and restaurants.

Requirements

The most important requirement is German language proficiency (at least B1, ideally B2), as instruction at the vocational school and communication at work will be in German. You also need a minimum 12th-grade high school certificate.
